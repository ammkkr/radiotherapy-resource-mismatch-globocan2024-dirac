# Additional Stability Analyses

## Burden-Version Update With a Common Baseline

- Both burden versions used 2025 as the baseline and 2050 as the horizon across 149 common matched countries.
- GCO version 2022 identified 24 countries and version 2024 identified 24.
- Retained across versions: 23.
- New in version 2024: PNG.
- No longer screen-positive in version 2024: ZWE.
- The same latest-reported DIRAC resource snapshot was fixed in both versions, so the common-year comparison isolates burden-data revision from baseline-year and resource changes.
- The legacy native-baseline comparison retained 22 countries and changed 4 classifications.

## Denominator and Threshold Sensitivity

- Using 2024 rather than 2050 selected-site cases in the MV-unit-density denominator retained 21 of 23 primary countries.
- Countries not retained under the 2024-density denominator: GTM, SYR.
- A stricter Q80 growth plus Q20 density definition retained 16 of 23 primary countries.
- The all-cancer burden definition retained 23 of 23 primary countries and identified 25 countries overall.

## DIRAC Record-Year Sensitivity

- Restricting to DIRAC records from 2023 or later retained 125 of 150 matched countries.
- 7 primary countries had older resource records: COD, ETH, MDG, NER, SDN, YEM, ZMB.
- Among 16 primary countries eligible for the restricted analysis, 16 were retained; not retained: none.
- Newly identified in the recent-record analysis: NAM, NIC, TGO.

## Missing-Resource Bounds

- Global country-level proportion: 12.4% to 21.5%.
- AFRO country-level proportion: 38.3% to 59.6%.
- Lower bounds treat all DIRAC-missing countries as screen-negative; upper bounds treat high-growth DIRAC-missing countries as meeting the resource criterion.
